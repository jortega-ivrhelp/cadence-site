Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

This policy explains how IVR Help ("we", "us") handles information on cxai.ivrhelp.com and in the CXAI service. Customer call data processed by CXAI is also governed by the customer's agreement with us, including any Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

Information you give us

When you request a pilot audit, join the waitlist, or contact us, we collect what you enter: name, work email, company, role, phone system, industry, number of locations, and your message. We use it only to respond to you and to discuss CXAI.

The interactive demo

The demo runs in your browser on simulated data. Nothing you click in the demo is sent to us. Files you load into the pilot audit tool are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (such as page views) to understand which pages are useful. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Customer call data

  • We process call data only to provide the service our customer contracted for, under their instructions.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to people who need it, and every access is logged.
  • Personal and payment details are redacted from transcripts wherever the service supports it.
  • Retention follows the customer's contract. At the end of the contract, data is returned or deleted.
  • We use a small set of sub-processors (for example, cloud hosting and speech-to-text providers), each bound by written data protection terms. The current list is available on request.
  • Protected health information is processed only after a BAA is signed.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run this website and our business, such as website hosting and Google Workspace for email and lead records. They may process information on our behalf only.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you gave us, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, which we will honor. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected parties of a breach as required by law and contract.

Children

CXAI is a business service and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes here and update the date above. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact options at www.ivrhelp.com.
