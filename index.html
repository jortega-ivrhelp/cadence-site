How Cadence improves your IVR/IVA

Cadence works alongside Genesys, NICE, Five9, Nextiva, Zendesk, 8x8, Cisco, and Google CCAI. It starts read-only and earns the right to change routing, one approved rule at a time.

  1. 01

    Connect read-only

    Your admin creates a read-only API key and adds a webhook. About 15 minutes, no routing changes.

  2. 02

    Call data flows in

    After every call, Cadence receives the event and recording, removes personal identifiers, and standardizes it.

  3. 03

    Find the problem

    Cadence spots where callers hang up, loop, or wait too long, by menu option, hour, and location.

  4. 04

    Recommend a fix

    It proposes a specific routing change, like an AI callback offer at lunch, with the expected impact.

  5. 05

    You approve

    Nothing changes without a named approver. Approved rules apply instantly and roll back in one click.

  6. 06

    Measure

    Before/after results land in role-based reports for leaders, supervisors, workforce, and admins.

Questions from IT and operations

Security & architecture

Get our one-page Security Roadmap. It covers encryption, PHI redaction, AI data-retention commitments, and the current status of each control.

Do we have to change phone systems or port numbers?
No. Cadence sits on top of your existing contact center platform (Genesys Cloud CX, NICE CXone, Five9, Nextiva, Zendesk Talk, 8x8, Cisco Webex Contact Center, or Google CCAI) and works with your current IVR or IVA. Numbers, carriers, and core routing stay as they are.
Does Cadence work with an IVA (intelligent virtual agent) as well as a traditional IVR?
Yes. Cadence analyzes and improves both touch-tone IVR menus and conversational IVAs. It finds where callers drop off or get stuck in either one, and its AI agent can take over routine intents your current IVR/IVA hands to staff today.
How does setup work?
Pilots start read-only. Your admin creates a scoped API key and adds a post-call webhook, which takes about 15 minutes and doesn't touch live routing. Live audio or an AI-handled IVR/IVA branch is turned on later, only for the branches you choose.
How do routing improvements get into our IVR/IVA?
Your admin adds one decision step to the IVR/IVA flow, once. After that, Cadence proposes specific changes (for example, an AI callback offer when Billing waits pass 90 seconds at lunch). Nothing changes until a named person approves it, and every change can be rolled back in one click.
What reports do supervisors and leaders get?
Role-based dashboards for contact center leaders, supervisors, workforce teams, and admins: AI containment, abandon trends, agent scorecards with coaching flags, interval staffing vs. forecast, compliance scores, and a full routing change log. Every view exports to CSV.
Will you sign a BAA or DPA?
Yes. Healthcare customers sign a Business Associate Agreement before any protected health information is processed, and every customer can sign a Data Processing Agreement. Our AI and hosting providers are covered by matching agreements.
Does Cadence work for my industry?
Cadence ships with ready-made templates for healthcare, financial services, insurance, retail and e-commerce, utilities, travel and hospitality, government, and home services. Each one pre-loads common intents, compliance checks, and team routing, and everything can be edited for your own call types.
What happens if we go over our included minutes?
Nothing stops working. Extra analyzed minutes are $30 per 1,000, and extra AI voice minutes are $0.15 each, or less with a prepaid bundle. You get an alert at 80% of your allowance, and we'll suggest the Scale plan if it would cost less.
What does the free pilot audit involve?
A 15-minute call plus a standard 30-day queue report exported from your platform (call counts only, no recordings or customer data). We return a fit score, your peak drop-off hours, and a conservative-to-upside ROI estimate.
Is the demo using real customer data?
No. The interactive demo runs entirely on simulated data for fictional organizations in each industry.
