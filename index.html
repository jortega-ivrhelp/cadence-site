One layer. Three jobs. Every call.

Automation, intelligence, and compliance run on the same pipeline. Every call that gets automated is also monitored and audited.

Pillar 1

Vertical AI automation

Takes over repetitive IVR branches such as rescheduling, intake, insurance checks, and hours. When a caller needs a person, it warm-transfers them back to your agents with full context.

  • Built for multi-location clinics
  • Warm handoff to your existing queues
  • Runs 24/7 with no added headcount
Pillar 2

Live intelligence & monitoring

Watches call flows across your platform for drop-off spikes, routing loops, and sentiment shifts. Each alert comes with a one-click fix.

  • IVR drop-off detection by branch and hour
  • Routing loop and dead-end detection
  • Sentiment trends by location
Pillar 3

Automated compliance auditing

Checks recordings and transcripts against HIPAA, TCPA, PCI-DSS, recording-consent rules, and your own SOPs, then produces audit-ready reports.

  • Covers every call, not a 1–2% sample
  • Flags missing disclosures and consent
  • Exportable reports by environment

Questions from IT and operations

Security & architecture

Get our one-page Security Roadmap. It covers encryption, PHI redaction, AI data-retention commitments, and the current status of each control.

Do we have to change phone systems or port numbers?
No. Cadence sits on top of your existing CCaaS platform. Numbers, carriers, and core routing stay as they are.
How does setup work?
Pilots start read-only. You create a scoped API key and add a post-call webhook, which takes minutes and doesn't touch live routing. Live audio streaming or an AI-handled IVR branch is turned on later, only for the branches you choose.
Will you sign a BAA?
Yes. A Business Associate Agreement is signed before any protected health information is processed, and our AI providers are covered by matching agreements.
Is the demo using real patient data?
No. The interactive demo runs entirely on simulated data for a fictional clinic group.
